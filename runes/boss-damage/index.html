RUNE ROUTE / BOSS DAMAGE

Task Bar Hero boss-damage Runes: target the check that stops you.

Use a single-target Rune route when a hard encounter, not routine waves, is the active limit on progression.

WHEN TO USE IT

Route toward boss damage only when the encounter is the real wall. Confirm that the test is repeatable before replacing a farm or survival priority.

Boss route method

  • Use the same target and build for each comparison.
  • Check damage timing and cooldown reliability, not just a peak result.
  • Record completion, time and any survival failure.
  • Review the route after gear or patch changes.

Boss damage versus reward evidence

Ver 1.02.03 added a Rune Node for Stage Boss Chest drop chance, but the announcement does not publish a Rune value table or say that the node increases boss damage. Keep the reward node in a separate column from damage, cooldown and survival results. This distinction matters especially in Plaguelands, where chest timing and stage-boss rewards changed across multiple patches.

BOSS TEST FIELDWHAT TO RECORDWHY
TargetAct, difficulty, stage and bossDifferent bosses create different checks.
Damage resultCompletion, time and failed attemptsSeparates a repeatable clear from a peak hit.
Cooldown resultSkill timing and round where it is availablePrevents a cooldown bug or delay being read as build power.
Reward nodeExact chest type and Rune node sourceDoes not confuse drop chance with boss damage.

Only replace a farming or survival route when the same boss becomes consistently easier under the recorded setup. A single successful attempt is a lead for testing, not a tier placement.
