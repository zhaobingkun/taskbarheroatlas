ABOUT / EDITORIAL METHOD

Task Bar Hero Atlas is an independent player guide.

This site organizes official update facts, practical testing methods and small browser tools for players who want a clearer next decision.

WHO WE ARE

Task Bar Hero Atlas is a fan-maintained reference site for TBH: Task Bar Hero. It is not operated by, sponsored by or affiliated with the developer, publisher or Steam. Game names and related marks belong to their respective owners.

What the site is for

The Atlas focuses on version-aware guides, Rune and gear decision frameworks, patch notes, source notes and lightweight tools. The goal is to help a player answer a concrete question: what changed, what is confirmed, what should be tested, and which route is sensible for the current account?

Some pages are reference hubs and some are practical methods. A hub points to a topic; a method explains how to compare a route; a patch page records what the developer actually announced. They should not be read as one universal ranking or as official game documentation.

How facts are checked

  1. Official first. Version numbers, release timing and bug-fix statements are checked against the official Steam Community news feed or official Steam pages.
  2. Evidence labels. Confirmed official facts, player observations and unresolved questions are kept separate.
  3. Version context. Current recommendations carry a patch or check date so an older screenshot is not presented as current.
  4. No invented numbers. If an official source does not publish a Rune value, drop percentage, reward total or best route, the Atlas says that the value is unverified instead of filling the gap with a guess.

What we do not promise

The Atlas does not promise a universal best build, permanent tier list, live market price, complete drop table or guaranteed farming route. Those claims require current inputs and repeatable tests. A player can use the supplied checklists and record a result for their own stage, build and patch.

Corrections and updates

Patch pages are refreshed when a new official announcement changes the current version or materially affects player decisions. Historical announcements remain visible as history rather than being rewritten as current facts. When evidence is incomplete, the page keeps the uncertainty visible and avoids turning a planned feature into a released one.

Privacy and external sources

The site does not require player accounts, game-save uploads or payment details. See the Privacy page for current analytics and third-party service details. External links lead to their own sites and policies; official sources are linked so readers can verify important claims themselves.
