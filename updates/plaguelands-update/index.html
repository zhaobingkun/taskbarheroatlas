Task Bar Hero Plaguelands update: what the official dev note says.

The Aug 19 Steam Community post previews a planned early-September update with a new stage type, higher-level items, more Runes and a Skin System. These features are announced as in development, not as live v1.01.05 content.

SHORT ANSWER

The official note says the September update is being prepared and may still change. It lists Plaguelands, level 90 items with about 20 new Unique Stats, new Rune content and a Skin System. The current released patch remains v1.01.05.

How Plaguelands is described in the dev note

The post says Corruption accumulates in proportion to play time and can also come from corroding items in the Cube. While Corruption remains above zero, players can fight stronger Plaguelands enemies. Players choose a Plague Intensity; higher intensity means stronger monsters and better treasure chests, with multiple tiers that unlock in sequence after clearing lower tiers.

The note also mentions Corrupted Soulstones obtained in Plaguelands, which can be used to challenge Plagued Act Bosses. These are official feature descriptions, not a published farming formula or guaranteed reward table.

What this means for current guides

  1. Keep v1.01.05 as the live baseline. The dev note does not announce a new released version.
  2. Do not pre-rank the new content. No complete Rune values, item stats, drop rates or best builds were published.
  3. Recheck the patch desk when the update lands. Stage access, Corruption behavior, rewards and balance should be updated from the final notes or repeatable tests.
  4. Keep historical and planned content separate. The September timing is a development target, not a confirmed launch timestamp.
