Task Bar Hero Plaguelands guide: v1.02.00 release through Ver 1.2.4 hotfix.

The official Sep 7 release and follow-up hotfixes establish Plaguelands access rules and current chest status. Ver 1.2.4 fixes stage-change and relaunch waiting-time resets and replaces fixed minimum waits with a total-per-period system. Use repeatable tests before calling a route or build best.

SHORT ANSWER

Ver 1.2.4 is the latest official hotfix. It fixes chest waiting-time resets after changing stages (including Plaguelands difficulty) and after relaunch, then removes fixed minimum waits between chest acquisitions. The official note does not state the numeric total allowed in a period. Earlier v1.02.00-v1.02.03 access, Rune and balance facts remain historical context.

How to enter Plaguelands

The official release notes say Plaguelands is accessed through the Portal UI on Nightmare, Hell and Torment after clearing Stage 1-10 of each difficulty. Entering requires at least 30 Contamination, which is consumed while inside and recharges over time online or offline.

Hotfix status for Plaguelands

v1.02.01 fixed intermittent Plaguelands progress resets and sent the official Plague Fruit compensation by mail. v1.02.02 then fixed unpurchased-DLC equipment appearing from Plaguelands chests, the Slayer Axe Spin cooldown issue and unpurchased DLC characters entering formations. Ver 1.2.4 later fixed chest waiting-time resets when changing stages, including Plaguelands difficulty, and the wait that had been applied after relaunching.

What to check before changing a build

Plaguelands update FAQ

What is the current Task Bar Hero version?

The latest official hotfix is Ver 1.2.4. It fixes chest waiting-time resets after stage changes and relaunches and removes fixed minimum waits between chest acquisitions.

What did Ver 1.2.4 change about Plaguelands chest timing?

It fixed stage-change and relaunch waiting-time resets and removed the fixed minimum wait between chest acquisitions. It describes a total-chests-per-period system but gives no numeric limit; v1.02.03 minimums are historical.

Is the stage-change chest waiting-time reset fixed?

Yes. Ver 1.2.4 says it fixed resets when changing stages, including Plaguelands difficulty, and fixed the wait that was always applied after relaunching.

How do the Sep 8 known issues relate to v1.02.01?

v1.02.01 records fixes for the stat display, Nightmare lock, Sanctuary healing, Contamination text and Plaguelands progress reset issues. It does not establish that every Plague Zone translation is correct. Ver 1.2.4 later reports fixes for chest waiting-time resets after stage changes and relaunches.

How do I enter Plaguelands?

Use the Portal UI on Nightmare, Hell or Torment after clearing Stage 1-10 of that difficulty, and have at least 30 Contamination to enter from a normal area.

Are Plaguelands drop rates fully known?

No. The official notes do not provide a complete universal drop table or a numeric total-chest limit for a period. Keep observed samples separate from official facts.
