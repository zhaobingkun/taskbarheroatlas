Task Bar Hero Plaguelands guide: v1.02.00 release through v1.02.03 fixes.

The official Sep 7 release, Sep 8 notices and follow-up hotfixes establish the current Plaguelands rules and issue status. Use this page for confirmed access and chest facts; wait for repeatable tests before calling a route or build best.

SHORT ANSWER

v1.02.03 is current. v1.02.00 adds the Plaguelands area and systems, v1.02.01 and v1.02.02 fix post-release issues, and v1.02.03 adds chest-drop Rune nodes, shorter selected timers and three bug fixes.

How to enter Plaguelands

The official release notes say Plaguelands is accessed through the Portal UI on Nightmare, Hell and Torment after clearing Stage 1-10 of each difficulty. Entering requires at least 30 Contamination, which is consumed while inside and recharges over time online or offline.

What to check before changing a build

  1. Attach the v1.02.03 label. Do not mix pre-update results with current Plaguelands results.
  2. Record the intensity and Contamination state. These conditions change the encounter and reward context.
  3. Keep chest type and difficulty visible. The v1.02.03 minimums apply only to the named chest categories and difficulties.
  4. Compare the same activity repeatedly. New Runes, Unique Stats and hotfixes need measured tests before a ranking is useful.
  5. Keep chest and market evidence separate. The official notes changed chest composition and later hotfixes reported specific chest-related issues.

Plaguelands update FAQ

What is the current Task Bar Hero version?

The current released patch is v1.02.03. It adds Plaguelands chest-drop Rune nodes, shortens selected minimum chest timers and fixes chest opening, resistance and launch issues.

What are the v1.02.03 Plaguelands chest minimums?

Normal Chests on Nightmare use a 4-minute minimum, down from 5. Stage Boss Chests use a 6-minute minimum on Nightmare and 7 minutes on Hell and Torment, down from 8.

Is the difficulty-change chest timer reset fixed?

No. The v1.02.03 announcement says that issue will be fixed in an upcoming update; it does not provide a date or version.

How do the Sep 8 known issues relate to v1.02.01?

v1.02.01 records fixes for the stat display, Nightmare lock, Sanctuary healing, Contamination text and Plaguelands progress reset issues. It does not establish that every Plague Zone translation is correct, and it separately reports a chest-respawn issue still under investigation.

How do I enter Plaguelands?

Use the Portal UI on Nightmare, Hell or Torment after clearing Stage 1-10 of that difficulty, and have at least 30 Contamination to enter from a normal area.

Are Plaguelands drop rates fully known?

The official notes describe relative chest behavior and reward changes, but they do not provide a complete universal drop table. Keep observed samples separate from official facts, including the separately reported chest respawn issue.
