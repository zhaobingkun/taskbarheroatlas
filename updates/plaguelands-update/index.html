Task Bar Hero Plaguelands guide: v1.02.00 release and v1.02.02 fixes.

The official Sep 7 release, Sep 8 known-issues notice and follow-up hotfixes establish the current Plaguelands rules and issue status. Use this page for confirmed access facts; wait for repeatable tests before calling a route or build best.

SHORT ANSWER

v1.02.02 is current. v1.02.00 adds the Plaguelands area, Level 90 items, new Unique Stats, Rune nodes, Statistics and Appearance systems, while v1.02.01 and v1.02.02 fix post-release issues.

How to enter Plaguelands

The official release notes say Plaguelands is accessed through the Portal UI on Nightmare, Hell and Torment after clearing Stage 1-10 of each difficulty. Entering requires at least 30 Contamination, which is consumed while inside and recharges over time online or offline.

Hotfix status for Plaguelands

v1.02.01 fixed intermittent Plaguelands progress resets and sent the official Plague Fruit compensation by mail. v1.02.02 then fixed unpurchased-DLC equipment appearing from Plaguelands chests, the Slayer Axe Spin cooldown issue and unpurchased DLC characters entering formations.

Unresolved-status boundary: v1.02.01 also reported that chests may fail to respawn after a fast clear followed by a stage change within five minutes. These official hotfix posts do not confirm that separate issue as resolved.

How the known-issues notice maps to the hotfixes

The Sep 8 notice reported over-maximum stat display, Plague Zone translation problems, a Nightmare lock, incorrect Sanctuary healing and a Plaguelands progress-reset condition. v1.02.01 later lists fixes for the display issue, Nightmare lock, Sanctuary healing, Contamination text and progress reset. The broader translation status is not fully established, and the separate fast-clear chest-respawn issue remains unresolved in the latest official posts.

What to check before changing a build

  1. Attach the v1.02.02 label. Do not mix pre-update results with current Plaguelands results.
  2. Record the intensity and Contamination state. These conditions change the encounter and reward context.
  3. Compare the same activity repeatedly. New Runes, Unique Stats and hotfixes need measured tests before a ranking is useful.
  4. Keep chest and market evidence separate. The official notes changed chest composition and later hotfixes reported specific chest-related issues.

Plaguelands update FAQ

What is the current Task Bar Hero version?

The current released patch is v1.02.02. v1.02.00 added Plaguelands, and the Sep 8 hotfixes address post-release issues.

How do the Sep 8 known issues relate to v1.02.01?

v1.02.01 records fixes for the stat display, Nightmare lock, Sanctuary healing, Contamination text and Plaguelands progress reset issues. It does not establish that every Plague Zone translation is correct, and it separately reports a chest-respawn issue still under investigation.

How do I enter Plaguelands?

Use the Portal UI on Nightmare, Hell or Torment after clearing Stage 1-10 of that difficulty, and have at least 30 Contamination to enter from a normal area.

Are Plaguelands drop rates fully known?

The official notes describe relative chest behavior and reward changes, but they do not provide a complete universal drop table. Keep observed samples separate from official facts, including the separately reported chest respawn issue.
