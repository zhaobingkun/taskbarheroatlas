PLAGUELANDS GUIDE / CHECKED OCT 28, 2026

Task Bar Hero Plaguelands guide: entry, chests and Relics in Ver 1.3.0.

Enter through the Portal UI on Nightmare, Hell or Torment after clearing Stage 1-10 in that difficulty, with at least 30 Contamination. The Oct 21 Ver 1.3.0 update improves Plaguelands chests and adds Relics; the changes below are official facts, not a tested best farming route.

SHORT ANSWER

Ver 1.3.0 remains the content baseline for the chest and Relic facts below. The latest official client checked on October 28 is Ver 1.3.2. Plaguelands Hell/Torment Common Treasure Chest timing changes from 5 to 4 minutes and Stage Treasure Chest timing from 7 to 6 minutes. Relative chest-drop chances also increase, and clearing Plaguelands once unlocks the Relic UI. These notes do not provide absolute drop percentages or guarantee a chest on a fixed schedule.

How do I unlock Relics through Plaguelands?

Clear Plaguelands once to unlock the Relic UI. Contaminated Reliquaries and Contaminated Relics can drop from Plaguelands chests and Contaminated Act Boss chests; the official note does not give an absolute chance. Use Cube Offering to convert a contaminated item into a random Reliquary or Relic.

Reliquary inherent stats apply to the party. Uncommon and higher Reliquaries have colored slots: match the Relic color, while a White Relic fits any color. Gold-based Attunement rerolls Reliquary slot colors or a Relic's inherent-stat value; costs rise to a cap, but the announcement does not publish the exact cost table. See the gear reference before comparing an upgrade.

Is the Halloween stage the same as Plaguelands?

No. Jack-o'-Lantern Canyon requires Stage 3-10 cleared on the matching Nightmare, Hell or Torment difficulty and consumes one matching Jack-o's Ticket. It is not the regular Plaguelands Stage 1-10 / 30 Contamination entry rule. Each entry offers up to 10 regular Halloween chests; defeating the boss awards one boss chest; storage is separate (40 regular and 40 boss), and Rune effects do not apply there. The event ends November 11, 2026 at 23:59 UTC. Check the event details and separate mailbox-gift deadlines.

How to enter Plaguelands

The official release notes say Plaguelands is accessed through the Portal UI on Nightmare, Hell and Torment after clearing Stage 1-10 of each difficulty. Entering requires at least 30 Contamination, which is consumed while inside and recharges over time online or offline.

Hotfix status for Plaguelands

v1.02.01 fixed intermittent Plaguelands progress resets and sent the official Plague Fruit compensation by mail. v1.02.02 then fixed unpurchased-DLC equipment appearing from Plaguelands chests, the Slayer Axe Spin cooldown issue and unpurchased DLC characters entering formations. Ver 1.2.4 later fixed chest waiting-time resets when changing stages, including Plaguelands difficulty, and the wait that had been applied after relaunching.

Plaguelands update FAQ

What is the current Task Bar Hero version?

The latest official client checked on October 28, 2026 is Ver 1.3.2. Plaguelands chest timing, relative multipliers and Relic unlocks remain the October 21 Ver 1.3.0 content baseline. Ver 1.3.1 and Ver 1.3.2 do not replace those figures or publish absolute drop rates.

What changed about Plaguelands chest timing in Ver 1.3.0?

For Hell and Torment, Common Treasure Chest timing changes from 5 to 4 minutes and Stage Treasure Chest timing from 7 to 6 minutes. These are the official timing labels, not a guaranteed universal chest schedule.

Are Plaguelands absolute drop rates known?

No complete absolute drop table is supplied. Ver 1.3.0 publishes relative multipliers: Nightmare Common 1.75×, Hell Common 2.5×, Hell Stage 1.5×, Torment Common 3× and Torment Stage 1.66×.

How do I enter Plaguelands?

Use the Portal UI on Nightmare, Hell or Torment after clearing Stage 1-10 of that difficulty, with at least 30 Contamination to enter from a normal area.

How do I unlock the Relic UI?

Clear Plaguelands once. Contaminated Reliquaries and Relics can drop from Plaguelands and Contaminated Act Boss chests, but their absolute drop chances are not stated.

Does Halloween use the same entry requirement?

No. Jack-o'-Lantern Canyon requires Stage 3-10 cleared on the matching difficulty and one matching Jack-o's Ticket. It is separate from the regular Plaguelands entry rule.

What did the older Ver 1.2.4 hotfix fix?

It fixed chest waiting-time resets after stage changes and relaunches and removed fixed minimum waits between chest acquisitions. That is historical context, not a substitute for the Ver 1.3.0 difficulty-specific changes.
