EVENT GUIDE / CHECKED OCT 28, 2026

Task Bar Hero Halloween event: what to farm before it ends.

Jack-o'-Lantern Canyon remains available through November 11, 2026 at 23:59 UTC. This guide separates the active event stage from the earlier October mailbox gift campaign and the Ver 1.3.2 ticket-drop fix.

Check the deadline →Open patch desk
EVENT DEADLINE

November 11, 2026 at 23:59 UTC. After that time, the official announcement says players can no longer enter Jack-o'-Lantern Canyon or obtain Jack-o's Tickets and Jack-o' Candy. The two-pet unlocks also require event monsters, so do not postpone those goals.

How to enter Jack-o'-Lantern Canyon

  1. Clear Stage 3-10 in the difficulty you want to enter.
  2. Open the Portal UI and choose Nightmare, Hell or Torment Canyon.
  3. Spend one Jack-o's Ticket matching that difficulty.
  4. Open event chests before the separate event storage reaches its limit.

Both monster types appear only in Jack-o'-Lantern Canyon. The pets can remain part of your collection after the event, but the official note does not promise a post-event way to unlock them.

What changed in Ver 1.3.2?

The latest hotfix fixed Halloween event dungeon tickets so that only tickets matching the appropriate difficulty can drop. It also moved the previously assigned lower-tier ticket chance to the matching difficulty. Use this rule when comparing new ticket results; it is not evidence of a new absolute drop rate.

Do not confuse the event with the October mailbox gifts

The separate surprise-gift campaign ran from October 22 at 05:00 UTC through October 25 at 04:59 UTC. It awarded a Torment Event Stage Entry Ticket and a Plague Fruit each day, but those gifts were mailbox rewards, not redeem codes, and unclaimed rewards could not be restored.

Is the Halloween event worth doing?

It is the only safe conclusion that the event offers time-limited access and collection progress. Prioritize the event if you want the two pets, event materials or a chance to use the event chests before the deadline. Do not assume it is the best EXP, gold or gear route for every account; the official announcement does not publish a universal efficiency ranking.

Halloween Event FAQ

When does the Task Bar Hero Halloween event end?

Jack-o'-Lantern Canyon, Jack-o's Tickets and Jack-o' Candy end on November 11, 2026 at 23:59 UTC.

What do I need to enter Halloween Canyon?

Clear Stage 3-10 in the matching difficulty and spend one Jack-o's Ticket of that difficulty. The event supports Nightmare, Hell and Torment.

Can I still claim the October surprise gifts?

No. The separate mailbox gift campaign ended on October 25 at 04:59 UTC. It was not a redeem-code campaign.

Does Ver 1.3.2 publish the Halloween ticket drop rate?

No. Ver 1.3.2 changes which difficulty-matching tickets can drop, but the official announcement does not give an absolute ticket percentage.
