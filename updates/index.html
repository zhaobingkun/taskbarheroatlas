Task Bar Hero v1.02.02 hotfix notes: what changed?

The official Steam feed records a Sep 8 known-issues notice followed by two hotfixes after the Plaguelands release. This page tracks v1.02.02 as current, records the v1.02.01 fixes and compensation, and keeps earlier release history separate.

CURRENT OFFICIAL PATCH

v1.02.02 is the current released patch tracked by Atlas. The official Sep 8 hotfix fixes three post-Plaguelands issues; the prior v1.02.01 hotfix addressed progress resets and related UI, skill and access bugs.

What the Sep 8 known-issues notice means

The official notice listed five post-v1.02.00 problems: over-maximum Cooldown Reduction and Block Chance display, incorrect Plague Zone translations, Nightmare Plague Zone appearing locked, Sanctuary healing at an incorrect value, and Plaguelands progress resets when entering a lower difficulty while progressing through Torment. v1.02.01 later states that it fixed the display issue, Nightmare lock, Sanctuary healing, Contamination text and Plaguelands progress reset. That later hotfix does not establish that every Plague Zone translation is correct, and its separate fast-clear chest-respawn issue remains an open boundary.

What the Aug 28 Error 404 notice confirms

The official notice says that some accounts are currently experiencing an Error 404 when launching the game. If the message includes text such as Error: 404, user not found, the development team asks affected players to submit a support request through the official Zendesk link.

The official development note previews four planned features: the Plaguelands stage type, level 90 items with about 20 kinds of new Unique Stats, new Rune types plus additions to existing types, and a Skin System. What players should do after the v1.01.05 update

  1. Confirm the in-game version. Use v1.01.05 before comparing a route or reporting a result.
  2. Update before opening chests or claiming important mail. The official note specifically includes a chest crash and a mailbox item-delivery issue on certain accounts.
  3. Keep save evidence intact. The announcement says backups are created every 24 hours and retained for up to 30 days; it does not state a manual restore workflow.
  4. Re-test affected guidance. A bug fix is not automatically a balance proof; update a build, market note or route only after the relevant behavior is checked.

Updates FAQ

What is the current Task Bar Hero version?

The current released patch is v1.02.02. The Sep 8 hotfix fixes three post-Plaguelands issues, while v1.02.01 records the earlier progress-reset fix and compensation.

What did the Sep 8 known-issues notice report?

It reported over-maximum stat display, Plague Zone translation and Nightmare-lock problems, incorrect Sanctuary healing, and a lower-difficulty progress-reset issue. v1.02.01 later records fixes for the display, lock, healing, Contamination text and reset issues; it does not prove every translation is correct.

What happened to the Plaguelands progress reset?

The official v1.02.01 notice says it was fixed and describes Plague Fruit compensation. The same notice separately says a fast-clear, stage-change chest respawn issue was still being investigated.

Does v1.02.02 prove a new best build or farming route?

No. The hotfixes fix specific bugs but do not provide enough testing to support a universal ranking or best stage.

What should I do about the Error 404 launch notice?

If your launch message includes text such as “Error: 404, user not found,” use the official Zendesk support portal linked in the notice. The announcement does not state a general workaround or resolution time.

Why does this page use a check date?

Game news moves quickly. The patch version, source and last-check date make it clear what was verified, rather than implying that every page is timeless.
