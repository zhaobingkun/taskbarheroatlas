Halloween: entry rules and event end

Jack-o'-Lantern Canyon opens through the Portal on Nightmare, Hell and Torment after clearing Stage 3-10 of the matching difficulty. Each entry consumes one matching Jack-o's Ticket. Tickets have a low chance to come from normal or Plaguelands Treasure Chests and Act Boss chests; the Ver 1.3.0 note does not give absolute ticket rates. Ver 1.3.2 adds that only tickets matching the appropriate difficulty can drop, and that the chance previously assigned to lower-tier tickets is added to the appropriate-difficulty chance. That is still not an absolute rate.

Each entry offers up to 10 regular Halloween chests; defeating the boss awards one boss chest. Separate event storage holds 40 regular and 40 boss chests; Rune effects do not apply. Auto-retry does not proceed when the required chest storage is insufficient. The event ends November 11, 2026 at 23:59 UTC; after the end, entry, ticket and candy acquisition stop. This is not the daily gift deadline.

The new Pumpkin Candy and Pumpkin Ghost pet requirements and bonuses are recorded on the Pets page.

Separate daily mailbox gifts: October 22–24 (ended)

This claim window has ended. The separate official surprise-gift announcement offered one Event Stage Entry Ticket (Torment) and one Plague Fruit (Arcana) each day on October 22, 23 and 24. They were Mailbox gifts, not redeem codes, and the items cannot be traded.

Each day's claim window is 05:00 UTC to 04:59 UTC the next day. The overall period is October 22, 2026 at 05:00 UTC through October 25 at 04:59 UTC. Unclaimed gifts cannot be restored. Do not confuse this with the November 11 Halloween stage end.

Updates FAQ

What is the current Task Bar Hero version?

The latest official client checked on October 28, 2026 is Ver 1.3.2, posted October 27. Ver 1.3.1 was posted October 26. Relics, Halloween, Plaguelands chest changes and EXP Runes remain the October 21 Ver 1.3.0 content baseline.

Do Ver 1.3.1 and Ver 1.3.2 publish new drop rates or a best build?

No. Both are bug-fix hotfixes. Ver 1.3.2 says Halloween dungeon tickets drop only for the matching difficulty and moves the previous lower-tier ticket chance onto that difficulty. It does not publish an absolute drop percentage, a numeric Rune level cap, or a best build.

Did the October 27 maintenance finish?

Yes. BACKND maintenance on October 27, 2026 ran from 01:00 UTC and finished at 04:30 UTC, earlier than the 05:00 UTC end in the advance notice. Compensation is Plague Fruit (Arcana) ×1 and Giant Jack-o Candy (Legendary) ×3. Mail may be delayed by up to 24 hours.

What changed to Plaguelands chest timing?

Ver 1.3.0 changes Hell/Torment Common Treasure Chest timing from 5 to 4 minutes and Stage Treasure Chest timing from 7 to 6 minutes, with separate relative drop boosts. These values are not a guaranteed universal schedule; the Ver 1.2.4 system description below is historical.

Is the stage-change chest waiting-time reset fixed?

Yes. Ver 1.2.4 says it fixed waiting-time resets when changing stages, including Plaguelands difficulty. It does not state a numeric total-chest limit.

What did the Sep 8 known-issues notice report?

It reported over-maximum stat display, Plague Zone translation and Nightmare-lock problems, incorrect Sanctuary healing, and a lower-difficulty progress-reset issue. v1.02.01 later records fixes for the display, lock, healing, Contamination text and reset issues; it does not prove every translation is correct.

Does Ver 1.2.8 prove a new best build or farming route?

No. The v1.2.8 hotfix describes a Synthesis recipe fix, but does not provide enough evidence for a universal farming route or ranking.

What should I do about the Error 404 launch notice?

If your launch message includes text such as “Error: 404, user not found,” use the official Zendesk support portal linked in the notice. The announcement does not state a general workaround or resolution time.

What did the historical October 8 server notice say?

The October 8 notice marked that issue resolved. It is not a current uptime guarantee. The later October 27 maintenance is a separate, completed window.

Why does this page use a check date?

Game news moves quickly. The patch version, source and last-check date make it clear what was verified, rather than implying that every page is timeless.
